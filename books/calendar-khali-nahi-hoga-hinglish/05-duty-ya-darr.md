"Mummy akeli hain, trip cancel kar dete hain," Devika ne husband se kaha. Mummy healthy thi, same society mein sister rehti thi, aur Devika roz do baar call karti thi. Phir bhi three-day trip ka thought aate hi chest tight. Bachpan se sentence suna tha: "Beti hi maa ka asli sahara hoti hai." Care real tha; test endless.

Duty aur guilt dono aapse kuch karwate hain, par unka texture alag. Duty specific hoti hai: medicine pickup, monthly budget, emergency plan. Guilt vague rehta hai: achhi beti hoti toh aur karti. Duty limits discuss kar sakti hai. Guilt har limit ko character verdict banata hai.

Devika ne mother ko project nahi banaya. Usne poocha, "Trip ke teen din mein tumhe actual support kya chahiye?" Mother ne kaha evening walk ke liye neighbour aur ek grocery order. Devika surprise hui. Uske dimaag ka demand mother ki demand se bada tha. Sister backup thi. Trip par bhi ek call daily agreed hua, constant location tracking nahi.

Kabhi actual duty heavy hoti hai. Dementia, disability, financial crisis ya child care spreadsheet se light nahi hota. Guilt distinction load erase nahi karti. Woh bas yeh poochne deti hai ki kaunsa work care hai, kaunsa social performance, aur kaunsa load fairly redistribute hona chahiye.

Gender aur caste duty language ko unequal banate hain. Bahu ki seva, eldest son's earning, younger woman's event labour, junior employee ka emotional cleanup "nature" nahi. Tradition kisi arrangement ka history bata sakti hai; consent prove nahi.

Duty test ke four questions: Harm kya prevent karna hai? Kya main uniquely responsible hoon? Minimum reliable action kya hai? Review kab? Guilt ka question alag hota hai: log mere baare mein kya kahenge? Woh information ho sakti hai, decision rule nahi.

Devika trip se enlightened wapas nahi aayi. Second night mummy ne call miss kiya toh panic hua. Five minutes baad callback aaya; phone silent tha. Anxiety fact nahi bani. Care kam nahi hua. Bas care ko apni sincerity prove karne ke liye constant suffering ki zaroorat nahi rahi.

Trip ke baad mother ne ek unexpected complaint ki: daily check-in kabhi concern se zyada monitoring lagta tha. Devika hurt hui; calls uske liye love the. Dono reasonable the. Unhone emergency and companionship needs separate ki. Mother neighbour ke walking group mein regular hui, Devika ke calls alternate evenings. Care recipient ki autonomy bhi duty ka part hai.

Guilt sometimes grief ka cover hota hai. Ageing parent ko fully safe rakhna impossible accept karne ke badle child extra tasks karta rehta hai. Har visit, test and call uncertainty remove nahi karte. Action useful limit tak; beyond that control fantasy. Yeh distinction emotionally easy nahi.

Duty shared karte waqt competence and relationship matter. Sibling money de sakta, local cousin appointments, paid worker daily support. Equal hours only fairness model nahi. Lekin contribution discussion transparent ho, gender default nahi. Jis person ke paas money kam hai uska time automatically free bhi nahi.

If mother genuinely high-risk hoti aur backup unavailable, trip cancel reasonable hota. Same action fear se bhi, care se bhi aa sakta hai. Decision ka meaning motivational quote decide nahi karta; facts, alternatives and repeated pattern karte hain.

Devika ne ek month ke liye guilt phrases note kiye. "Log kya kahenge," "unhone tumhare liye itna kiya," "achhi beti," aur apna silent version: "Agar kuch hua toh meri galti." List blame collect karne ke liye nahi; demand ko practical language mein translate karne ke liye. "Achhi beti" ka answer impossible tha. "Tuesday doctor visit kaun karega?" discuss ho sakta tha. Jab moral fog task mein badli, kuch duty usne li, kuch sister, kuch mother. Har emotional claim task nahi banta; har task love ka final exam bhi nahi.

Mother kabhi indirect line phir bhi bol deti: "Aajkal bachche busy hain." Devika ne har sentence correct nahi kiya. Relationship courtroom ban jaata toh care ka texture khatam. Usne pattern aur actual request alag rakhe. Sometimes she called extra because she wanted to, and did not convert that choice into future obligation. Freely given care tabhi free rehti hai jab previous generosity next yes ka compulsory deposit na bane.

Kuch guilt phir bhi raha. Goal usse completely remove karna nahi tha; guilt ke hote hue demand, choice aur limit ko alag pehchanna tha. Emotional discomfort decision ka automatic veto nahi.
