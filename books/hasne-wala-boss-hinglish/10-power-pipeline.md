Promotion ke first month director ne Isha ko message kiya: "Need names. Who caused the miss?" Indore SaaS company mein incident multiple failures ka tha—sales ne wrong promise, engineering estimate rushed, QA window half. Isha ne reply box mein junior developer Om ka naam type kiya. Bug uske code mein tha. Send se pehle use yaad aaya ki timeline approve usne ki thi.

Bad manager banna aksar villain decision se shuru nahi. Pressure pipeline hoti hai. Senior urgency neeche aati hai; manager context hata kar blame forward karta hai. Small shortcuts repeat hote hain: vague promise, favourite ko benefit, quiet employee ko ignore, weekend message, promotion hope, public correction.

Good intention system ke bina stress mein evaporate hoti hai. Isha ne incident note four lines mein banaya: events, decisions, constraints, repair. Om ka bug included, compressed review and her approval bhi. Director ko simple culprit nahi mila. Conversation harder hui, accurate bhi.

Manager safeguards practical hon. Decision log: kya, kyun, accepted risk. Work allocation view: high-visibility and cleanup tasks ka distribution. Promise record: career conversations with conditions and authority limits. Boundary default: delayed send, emergency definition. Feedback check: behaviour and impact, personality fog nahi. Appeal route: employee aapke boss ya HR se baat kar sake.

Public credit/private correction useful thumb rule hai, absolute nahi. Public misinformation ko public correct karna padta hai; safety breach team learning maangta hai. Person ko humiliate kiye bina fact restore ho sakta hai. Private feedback bhi vague threat nahi.

Fairness sameness nahi. Disabled employee accommodation favour nahi; caregiver schedule flexibility low commitment proof nahi. At the same time, hidden exceptions resentment create karte hain. Privacy preserve karte hue process and criteria clear rakho.

Manager ke paas bhi limited power ho sakti hai. Understaffing, impossible target and discriminatory senior chain ko breathing exercise solve nahi karti. Upward push, scope reduction, written risk, collective escalation or exit relevant. Lekin "mere hands tied" bolkar apna available choice deny mat karo. Kaunsi line aapne challenge ki, kya protect kiya, kya honestly nahi kar sake?

Apology specific ho: "Kal review mein maine tumhari baat kaat kar delay tum par daala. Timeline maine approve ki thi. Corrected note bhej raha hoon; next review mein incident order follow hoga." Sorry ke baad changed process, warna relief manager ko milta hai.

Isha ne Om ko hero nahi banaya; code review mein uski mistake address hui. Usne khud ko martyr bhi nahi banaya; staffing risk director ko diya. Accountability distribute hui, dilute nahi. Power ka ethical use soft hona nahi. Reality ko jis direction mein convenient ho us direction mein dhakelne se mana karna hai—even jab pipeline upar se full pressure mein aaye.

Quarter end brought a harder test. One team member had repeated misses despite clear goals and support. Isha feared becoming toxic if she used performance plan. Avoiding clear feedback would protect her self-image and leave teammates carrying work. She documented examples, asked for employee context, offered support and set exact review markers. Kindness employment consequence ko hide karna nahi.

Employee disclosed depression treatment and requested accommodation. Isha involved qualified HR process, adjusted meeting load and preserved core job expectations with review. She did not diagnose, promise cure or share details with team. Work redistribution communicated as temporary capacity plan. Health context changed support, not person's dignity.

Another employee asked why standards differed. Fair manager cannot reveal private medical information. Isha explained that accommodations exist and each performance case uses role requirements plus approved support. Some ambiguity remained. Privacy sometimes prevents full social proof of fairness; consistent process must carry trust.

Director ne baad mein Isha ko praise kiya kyunki incident quiet raha. Usne lesson correct kiya: quiet metric nahi tha; service restore hui, causes fix hue, Om learning ke saath retained raha, aur risk log update hua. Silent incident suppression bhi ho sakta hai. Woh chahti thi senior leadership process reward kare, visible conflict ki absence nahi.

Management power kabhi clean nahi hoti. Incomplete information wale decisions pay, health aur careers affect karte hain. Safeguards manager ko innocent nahi banate; choices ko inspect, challenge aur repair karne layak banate hain. Yeh "good boss" banne se kam glamorous, aur fake karne mein zyada difficult hai.

Isi jagah intention ko daily evidence milta hai.
