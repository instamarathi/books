"Yeh sirf tumhe bata rahi hoon," Pooja ne pantry mein kaha, "Kabir tumhare delays boss ko forward karta hai." Same afternoon Kabir ko message mila ki Pooja uske role par nazar rakhti hai. Kolkata insurance office mein manager Sandeep rarely direct accusation karta. Woh concern share karta: "Main tumhe protect kar raha hoon, bas careful rehna." Month end tak teammates documents password-protect kar rahe the.

Favourites ko simple "chamcha" kehna emotional satisfaction deta hai, analysis nahi. People adapt to incentives. Agar boss proximity se safety, promotion ya schedule milta hai, workers alliance banayenge. Kuch actively harm karte hain; kuch survive. Behaviour ki accountability rahe, context bhi.

Divide-and-rule ka pattern information asymmetry par chalta hai. Har person ko different story, private warning, comparison, scarcity. Direct conversation risky lagti hai kyunki boss ne trust already poison kiya. Team errors share nahi karti; manager central translator ban jaata hai.

Kabir ne Pooja ko confront karke confession demand nahi kiya. Usne specific workflow issue uthaya: claims status shared board par, delay reason visible, priority change written. Pooja skeptical thi, par shared data ne gossip ki authority kam ki. Dono friends nahi bane. Cooperation ke liye friendship necessary nahi.

Peer solidarity ka bhi risk hai. Group kisi colleague ko scapegoat bana sakta hai, harassment complaint suppress kar sakta hai, ya "boss ke against unity" ke naam par dissent punish. Collective story bhi evidence se test honi chahiye. Confidentiality aur consent important.

Team repair ke practical pieces: shared source of truth; rotating meeting roles; public allocation criteria; direct clarification before forwarding rumours; credit record; safe channel outside boss. Ye steps manipulative manager ko transform nahi karte. Bas uski monopoly kam kar sakte hain.

Power difference ignore mat karo. Permanent employee contract worker se "stand together" kahe toh downside same nahi. Higher-paid colleague risk zyada carry kar sakta hai. Solidarity equal sacrifice demand nahi; unequal safety ko plan mein include karti hai.

Pooja aur Kabir ne finally compare kiya toh pata chala dono ko almost same warning mili thi. Sandeep expose hokar office se nahi nikla. Bas agla private message aaya toh Kabir ne poocha, "Is concern ko Pooja ke saath shared thread par clarify kar dein?" Triangle ko teesri side mil gayi.

Shared board ke first month ek aur conflict surfaced: Pooja ke two delayed files the jo usne disclose nahi kiye the. Transparency ne kisi ek ko innocent aur dusre ko guilty prove nahi kiya. Usne client dependency explain ki aur one missed follow-up accept kiya. Reality conspiracy story se kam satisfying hoti hai. Direct data ne mixed accountability possible ki.

Sandeep started holding decisions in corridor conversations instead of board. Team agreed that work priority changes become active only after short channel note, except genuine emergency. This rule protected memory, but Sandeep could still affect ratings. Process reduced one lever, not total power.

Ek colleague ne shared discussions refuse kiye kyunki previous gossip ne usse harm kiya tha. Team ne use disloyal label nahi kiya. Usne sirf written status use kiya. Log individually burn hue hon toh psychological safety collectively demand nahi ki ja sakti. Participation alag speed se return kar sakti hai.

Peer group ne apni hierarchy bhi check ki. Senior employees solidarity claim karte hue low-risk tasks neeche forward kar rahe the. Contract staff weekend checks carry kar raha tha. Schedule redistribute hua aur contract constraints include hue, unhe complaint mein force kiye bina. Boss ki manipulation partly isliye chali kyunki existing inequality ne easy fault lines di thi.

Six months later Pooja aur Kabir professionally cautious rahe, best friends nahi bane. Healthy team ko emotional closeness required nahi. Use predictable information, punishment ke bina disagreement aur survival ke liye private rumour ke alawa routes chahiye.

Their team charter added a rumour rule: personal allegation forward nahi; affected person se direct clarification if safe, otherwise formal channel. "If safe" important tha. Har person ko alleged harasser confront karna required nahi. Work allocation rumour and misconduct report same process bhi nahi. One calls for clarification, another may need protection and investigation. By separating categories, team ne "no gossip" ko silence weapon banne se roka. Rules tab healthy hain jab powerful person ke conduct ko hide nahi karte.

Aur jab junior employee bhi unhe challenge kar sake.
