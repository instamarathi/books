Cover directions

C is the base direction. D, E, and F are three related variants we can mix across book folders.

A. Editorial Paperback

Light, literary, Marathi-bookstore feel. More readable and warmer, less app-like.

आत्म-विकास१काय खरंच मोलाचं आहे?
लेख२वाचून होत नाही

B. Premium Symbol

Dark, richer, more “book cover” than card. Each category can get one strong abstract symbol.

आत्म-विकासमिडलाइफ क्रायसिस रीडिझाईन
कारकीर्दनव्या भूमिकेत पहिले ९० दिवस

C. Modern Series

Most distinctive in the grid. Bold color blocking makes the bookshelf feel like a designed collection.

समाजसहा विचार टोप्या
घरनिटनेटके घर

D. Block Field

Cleaner and more literary than C, but still bold. Good for essay/self-reflection books.

आत्म-विकासमिडलाइफ क्रायसिस रीडिझाईन

E. Diagonal Band

Sharper and more energetic. Useful for career, society, thinking, and argument-heavy books.

F. Poster Split

Most graphic and shelf-visible. Works when we want the collection to feel designed and high-contrast.
