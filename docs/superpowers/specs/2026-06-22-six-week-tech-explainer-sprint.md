# 6-week sprint: a Marathi tech-explainer book

**Date:** 2026-06-22
**Owner:** Anup
**Status:** Active

## The goal behind the goal

This is a sabbatical project with three jobs, all done by one activity (writing in public):

1. **Pivot test** — does being a *writer/teacher* (software as raw material) actually energize me? The writing itself is the experiment.
2. **Reputation** — a finished, visible body of work: a short Marathi book on AI/tech, a field almost no one occupies in Marathi.
3. **Networking (introvert-mode)** — the work is the handshake. Inbound, not rooms.

Long-run framing: *pivot to something new* + *build independent reputation*, bridging English (tech cred, later) and Marathi (less crowded, closer to heart). This sprint is **Marathi-only** to protect focus.

## The artifact

An `essay`-kind book on the instamarathi site:

- **Working title:** हे सगळं कसं चालतं? — *How Does This Actually Work?*
- **Subject:** AI and the digital world, explained honestly (no hype, no jargon) for smart Marathi readers who feel left behind by it.
- **Author stance:** the friend who actually builds this stuff, telling you the truth about it.
- **Length:** 8 short essay chapters, ~900–1300 words each.

### Candidate chapter list (refine in Week 1)

1. AI तुमचं बोलणं कसं समजतं? — what an LLM actually does (intuition, no math)
2. AI "विचार" करतं का, की फक्त अंदाज? — the honest answer everyone wants
3. तुमचा data नक्की कुठे जातो? — what "free" apps actually take
4. Algorithm तुम्हाला का गुंतवून ठेवतं? — the feed, explained
5. Deepfake आणि खरं-खोटं — how not to get fooled
6. AI तुमची नोकरी घेईल का? — past the hype, what's real
7. Online पैसा — UPI, crypto, फसवणूक — money tech, demystified
8. AI चा शहाणपणाने वापर — practical close

## Schedule

- **Week 1 (discover + commit):** write 2 trial chapters (#1 and #6 — opposite ends, both bait-worthy). Lock title + final 8-chapter list by Sunday.
- **Weeks 2–5 (ship the spine):** 2 chapters/week, every **Tuesday + Friday**. Each is a finished essay the day it ships.
- **Week 6 (consolidate + launch):** polish, write the intro chapter, publish full book, one share-push, write a 1-page honest "what did this tell me about the pivot?" assessment.

## Introvert networking (woven in, zero events)

- Publishing is the net — each chapter into 2–3 Marathi WhatsApp/Telegram/Reddit communities the day it ships.
- Reply to everyone, initiate almost no one.
- Week 4: one small batch — message 2–3 Marathi creators/educators whose work overlaps. Specific, async, "I made this, thought you'd find it useful."

## Anti-laziness scaffolding

- Tuesday/Friday ship cadence (external deadline beats willpower).
- Visible counter: chapters shipped (x/8), readers, replies.
- 30-min Sunday review: what shipped, what's next, energized or dragging?

## Success on day 42

A finished 8-chapter Marathi book live on the site, ~8 shared pieces with real reader reactions, and a clear gut answer to *"is writing/teaching the thing?"*
