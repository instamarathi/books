# Midlife Redesign - English Design Spec

**Goal:** Create a new English-language essay book that reworks the ideas of `midlife-redesign` for Indian readers without relying on Maharashtra-only examples.

## Scope

- New book folder at `books/midlife-redesign-english/`
- `meta.json` with a new slug, English title, subtitle, credit line, and 12-chapter order
- 12 essay chapters in English
- Chapter voice stays practical and reflective, not self-help-generic
- Examples come from India broadly: offices, homes, schools, public transport, family obligations, and money stress across multiple regions and cities

## Content strategy

- Keep the core arc of the Marathi book: slowing down, noticing autopilot, learning to say no, revisiting old beliefs, using experience well, facing money fear, loneliness, self-relationship, parenting, and long-range thinking
- Reframe the material in natural Indian English rather than translating Marathi sentence patterns literally
- Use specific Indian settings and details, but avoid locking the book to any single state or city
- Preserve the essay form: one argument per chapter, no howto sections, no numbered technique lists

## Deliverables

- `books/midlife-redesign-english/meta.json`
- 12 Markdown chapter files with valid frontmatter
- Chapter order that matches the new English filenames

## Out of scope

- No change to the existing Marathi book
- No new app code or renderer changes
- No paywall, routing, or build-system changes
